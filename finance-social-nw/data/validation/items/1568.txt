The 66th issue of Uncanny Magazine, winner of eight Hugos, plus a British Fantasy Award, a Locus Award, and World Fantasy Award, will be available on September 2 at uncannymagazine.com.
Hugo Award-winning Publisher and Editor-in-Chief Michael Damian Thomas is proud to present the 66th issue of their eight-time Hugo Award-winning online science fiction and fantasy magazine, Uncanny Magazine. Stories from Uncanny Magazine have won Hugo, Nebula, Locus, Sturgeon, and World Fantasy Awards. As always, Uncanny features passionate SF/F fiction and poetry, gorgeous prose, provocative essays, and a deep investment in the diverse SF/F culture, along with a Parsec Award-winning monthly podcast featuring a story, poem, and interview from that issue.
All of Uncanny Magazine’s content will be available in eBook versions on the day of release from Weightless Books, Amazon, Barnes & Noble, Google Play, and Kobo. Subscriptions are always available through Weightless Books. The free online content will be released in two stages — half on the day of release and half on October 7.
Follow Uncanny on their website, or on Twitter, Bluesky, and Facebook.
