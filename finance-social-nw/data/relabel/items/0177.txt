A rental agreement is a legally binding contract signed by a landlord and tenant that outlines the details of a rental property. It generally includes the rental dates, requirements for paying rent, and expectations for both the landlord and tenant.
If you’re thinking of purchasing a home or apartment to rent out, there are standard lease agreements available online that can help you streamline this process. Read on for a guide.

Benefits of Printable Tenancy Agreements for Rental Property
If you’re navigating the leasing process for a new rental, here are some reasons to consider printable agreements:
- Protect your property: An agreement between a landlord and tenant helps each side understand the others’ expectations. You can outline things like the lease term and security deposits to protect your assets.
- Save time and money: Printable contracts allow you to avoid hiring a lawyer or creating your own document from scratch. Many of the agreements below cost just a few dollars.
- Customize to your needs: Printable rent agreements can often be edited. So you can input the specific details of your property, like the rent amount and what happens if the tenant violates rules in the rental unit.
- Stay compliant: Landlords are required to provide a safe living space and live up to the terms presented to a prospective tenant. With a printed agreement template, you can easily refer back to live up to your end of the deal.
- Rent units quickly: You can only make money from a rental property when a tenant rents your space. Lease agreement templates can be downloaded and printed right away, so they won’t hold up the rental process.

What should be included in a Rental Agreements Printable?
If you’re looking for a printable rental agreement or thinking of making your own, it’s essential to understand what should be included. Each form may vary based on its intended use, but here are some basics that most standard lease agreements cover:
- Names and signatures of landlords and tenants: Include a space where the parties sign and print their names.
- Description of the premises: The location, size, and type of property should be included.
- Dates of tenancy: For leases of a fixed period, include the start and end dates. For tenancy-at-will arrangements, include a start date and lease term, if relevant.
- Payment terms: Outline when the tenant is to pay rent, the rent amount, and accepted forms of payment.
- Deposits and fees: Include any other fees or deposits the tenant is responsible for, like security deposit funds that are held until the lease is up.
- Maintenance requirements: Outline who is responsible for maintaining what parts of their home, how frequently, and to what specific level of quality.
- Property rules: If there are any rules, like quiet hours or types of acceptable decor, outline them in the agreement.
What happens if you have no written rental agreement?
Laws differ by state, but written agreements are typically recommended or required to resolve legal disputes between landlords and tenants. Without a written agreement, both parties may need to go to court and rely on other forms of evidence to support their claims.
