Police appeal to find topless man who 'fled' after 'shouting for help'
Police officers could not find the man when they arrived at the scene of the incident
Police have released a CCTV image of a man they are looking to find. At 10.30pm on Friday, July 11, officers from Cheshire Police were called to reports of a man banging on windows on Mercer Street in Burtonwood, Warrington and shouting for help.
Officers attended the scene, but when they arrived there was no trace of the man. He is believed to have fled towards Gorsey Lane on the other side of the town, near Bruche. The man was described as white and of slim build.
At the time of the incident, he was topless and wearing dark trousers. Enquiries to locate him have been ongoing and officers are keen to hear from anyone with any information which may aid their investigation.
Detective Constable Philip Jackson, of Warrington CID, said: “Enquiries in relation to this incident have been ongoing and we’re keen to locate the man in the CCTV to ensure that he is safe and well.
“I’d ask anyone who recognises the man, or anyone with any information in relation to the incident, to contact the team here at Warrington.
“I would also like to appeal directly to the man in the CCTV image to ask him to get in touch so we can ensure you’re okay.”
Anyone with any information is asked to contact Cheshire Police on 101, quoting IML 2130301.
