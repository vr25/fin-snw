Pet Transport NZ Head Office Director's May 2026 Update:
We know this is an uncertain time for both humans and pets. Pet Transport NZ is still safely transporting our furry travellers all over the world. We are also working hard with our network of international agents to assist with transits via Auckland and New Zealand.
Flying pets to Australia, Asia, Canada, France, United Kingdom, USA and Pacific Islands remains unaffected.
If your pet's destination is not listed above, we have a very strong network of IPATA and Gem Network agents based around the world who can assist with onward movements and global shipments. We are closely monitoring fuel surcharges and updating our clients when necessary.
Emirates and Qatar Airways, airlines for pets moving from New Zealand to the Middle East, EU and parts of the UK are currently grounded. Qatar Airways anticipates full resumption of service by June 2026. Rest assured we are still flying pets around the world on a daily basis.
