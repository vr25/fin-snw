Alphabet Surges Nearly 7% After Berkshire Hathaway’s Big Bet
Alphabet stock jumped sharply in overnight trading. Shares of Google’s parent rose almost 7% after Warren Buffett’s Berkshire Hathaway revealed a $4.3 billion investment. The filing showed Alphabet becoming Berkshire’s tenth-largest U.S. equity holding. Google shares traded at $293.40 in after-hours data on Robinhood, up 6.1%, after touching a 7% high. The move comes even as valuations across major tech names face scrutiny due to heavy AI-driven rallies.
Apple Falls as Berkshire Trims $10.6 Billion in Shares
Apple stock dipped after Berkshire disclosed selling $10.6 billion worth of AAPL in the third quarter. Apple remains Berkshire’s largest position, but Buffett has steadily reduced the holding through 2025 while building cash. Berkshire also cut its stake in Bank of America. The strategic shift contrasts with the new Alphabet purchase, which Buffett once admitted he regretted not making earlier.
Alphabet recently reported stronger-than-expected earnings. The company posted $2.87 EPS, beating the $2.29 forecast. Revenue reached $102.35 billion, surpassing expectations of $99.90 billion.
