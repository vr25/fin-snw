The new Foodland grocery store under construction in Niagara-on-the-Lake is now scheduled to be open early next year.
Sobeys communications spokesperson Manasvi Thakur said in an emailed statement the 1,330-square-metre outlet in Garrison Village is proposed to open in early 2026.
“We will keep you posted as the date is confirmed,” said Thakur.
ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW
The grocery store, beside The Garrison House restaurant, had been projected to open before the Christmas holidays.
Work on the building began in late 2024. Thakur did not provide a reason why there is a delay in opening the store.
ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW
Construction is overseen by The Village Developments Inc.
The store will be owned and operated by Niagara Falls resident Lindsey Bridgeman, currently manager at Sobeys in Niagara Falls. Sobeys operates more than 215 Foodland stores in Canada.
ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW
The Niagara-on-the-Lake store is to have fresh food, meat and dairy products, as well as a deli, sushi bar and bakery section.
ARTICLE CONTINUES BELOW
Sobeys held a job fair at Niagara-on-the-Lake Community Centre in October and has nearly filled its labour needs, said officials on its Facebook site.
In an earlier interview, Sobeys said the outlet store would employ between 65 and 80 people.
