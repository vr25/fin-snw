Meta will soon offer paid versions of Facebook and Instagram in the UK that will remove advertising from both platforms.
In the coming weeks, users will be given the choice to pay £2.99 (A$6.12) a month to access ad-free versions of either service on the web, or £3.99 for the iOS or Android apps. Meta said it was charging more for access on apps because of subscription fees levied by Apple and Google on their respective app stores.
