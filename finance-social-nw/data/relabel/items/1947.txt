Broadcom shares rise 5% after earnings, despite skepticism among investors. Investors' expectations were especially lofty because of Broadcom's high stock valuation, says Jake Behan, head of capital markets at Direxion, making it a formidable competitor to Nvidia. Behan says Broadcom's $10 billion share buyback authorization is helping the stock after hours. Broadcom's revenue guidance, which is nearly $2 billion ahead of analysts' expectations, also helps boost confidence, as well as its projection for $10.7 billion in AI revenue in the current quarter. (katherine.hamilton@wsj.com)
Broadcom Inc. specializes in designing, developing, and selling analog components and sub-systems, with mixed and optoelectronic signals. The group's products include power amplifiers, radio-frequency filters, encoders, optocouplers, fiber-optic transmitters and receivers, etc.
