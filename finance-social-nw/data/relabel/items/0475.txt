Alleged serial sex offender suspected of murdering woman
Jennifer Foley's friend believes Richard Mantha murdered her
One of suspected serial sex offender Richard Mantha’s alleged victims believes he murdered her friend, court documents say.
In an affidavit provided by Det. Hendrik Bekkering that was released by the Crown following a media request, the officer details the Calgary Police Service’s ongoing investigation into Mantha.
Mantha, 59, faces multiple charges, including sexual assault and administering noxious substances, involving seven women police say were vulnerable members of the community.
One of those women came forward following a massive police raid on Mantha’s rental property east of Chestermere conducted over the Easter long weekend.
The search, which included the use of cadaver dogs, did not trigger any charges related to the discovery of human remains.
But in an interview with Acting Staff Sgt. Shelby Stewart, the woman detailed allegations that Mantha had repeatedly sexually assaulted her and expressed her suspicion he was involved in the death of Jennifer Ashley Foley, Bekkering said.
“Mantha wanted (the alleged victim) and her friend, Jennifer Foley . . . to sell drugs for him. (She) refused, but Jennifer agreed,” Bekkering wrote.
“Jennifer eventually owed Poncho (the nickname by which Mantha was known by sex-trade workers) $200 and he threatened to kill her,” Bekkering said the woman told police.
“Mantha moved to the Mountainview trailer park in December of 2020. Jennifer was reported missing shortly after and (she) suspects Mantha killed her. Jennifer’s body was found on a rural road near Chestermere.”
The woman also said a man named “Gary” told her Mantha killed Foley.
In January 2021, RCMP said the remains found dumped along Range Road 284 west of Chestermere were those of Foley.
At the time, Mounties said the cause of death had not been determined but the circumstances were suspicious.
The woman also alleged Mantha would force her to participate in sexual threesomes, which started after he moved to the trailer park and continued when he moved to the acreage.
“He would make her do things she didn’t want to do,” Bekkering wrote.
Mantha not suspected in presumed death of Krystal Russell
The investigation also focused on the disappearance of sex-trade worker Krystal (Trustice) Russell, whom police believe is dead after she hasn’t been seen or heard from since last New Year’s Eve.
But the Calgary Police Service said they have since eliminated Mantha as a suspect in Russell’s disappearance.
“CPS previously explored a possible connection between the disappearance of Krystal Russell and the Richard Mantha investigation,” police said in a news release.
“However, any connection between the two has since been ruled out.”
Police said the investigation into Russell’s disappearance is ongoing and ask that anyone with information about her contact them, or Crimestoppers.
None of the allegations against Mantha has been proven in court.
Mantha, who remains detained, is scheduled to face a three-week French-language trial in Calgary Court of Justice beginning Jan. 22.
