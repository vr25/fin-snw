Confirmed List of Products Launching at Xiaomi Smarter Living 2024
Xiaomi Smarter Living 2024 is all set to kick start on April 23 in India. The brand will be launching IoT devices such as a tablet, a pair of earbuds, one robot vacuum mop, and more.
The brand is gradually revealing the upcoming products along with their features. In this article, we have listed all the devices that are launching at the Xiaomi Smarter Living 2024.
Redmi Pad SE
The Redmi Pad SE is the upcoming tablet which is going to join Xiaomi's existing tablets available in the Indian market. It is confirmed to come with an 11-inch display, a 90Hz refresh rate and TUV Rheinland low blue light certification. The tablet will be powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 680 chipset.
Xiaomi also confirmed the device will have a quad-speaker setup with Dolby Atmos support. It will allow users to enjoy multitasking with Splitscreen and experience multiple applications at once. The Redmi Pad SE is claimed to provide a long-lasting battery life with 43 days of standby, 219 hours of music playback, and 14 hours of video playback time. For imaging, there will be a single rear camera and a front camera sensor.
The tablet is already available in the international market for EUR 199 (roughly Rs. 17,900). Given that, we are expecting the Redmi Pad SE will fall in the sub-20k segment.
Redmi Buds 5A
The Redmi Buds 5A will also be launching at Xiaomi Smarter Living 2024 event. The upcoming TWS earbuds are confirmed to come in black and white colour options. In terms of design, it will ship with a lightweight design along with a matte finish for the charging case. There will be 12mm dynamic drivers and Bluetooth 5.4 for connectivity. The earbuds will support ANC and Google fast pair technology.
Xiaomi Robot Vacuum Cleaner S10
Xiaomi is introducing Robot Vacuum Cleaner S10 at the event which will come with laser-based navigation, zig-zag and Y-shaped cleaning patterns, and multiple map memory features. It is also confirmed to deliver a turbo suction power of 4000 Pa.
Xiaomi Handheld Garment Steamer
Xiaomi's official site confirms its upcoming Handheld Garment Steamer will be safe for all kinds of fabrics and will deliver a steam power surge of up to 1300W. It will come with a steam rate of up to 24g/min and will be convenient to use both horizontally and vertically.
