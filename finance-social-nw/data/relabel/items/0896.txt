2024 Motor City Radio Club Field Day: Ham radio operators from the Motor City Radio Club will be participating in a national amateur radio exercise from 2 p.m. on Saturday June 22 until 2 p.m. Sunday, June 23 at Young Patriot’s Park in Riverview. The event is ARRL Field Day, an annual amateur radio activity organized since 1933 by ARRL, The National Association for Amateur Radio in the United States. Ham radio, also known as amateur radio, is a hobby and service that allows licensed individuals to communicate using designated radio frequencies. Amateur Radio is a licensed radio service. To earn a license requires passing examinations regulated by the Federal Communications Commission. Amateur radio exams will be offered during this year’s Field Day at 10 a.m. on Saturday, June 22 at the Riverview Veterans Memorial Library, 14300 Sibley Road. For more information, www.w8mrm.net
