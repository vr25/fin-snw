I try really hard not to work when I take time off. But, I do cruise through Facebook and some of the pages I follow as the Wetaskiwin Times, I follow on my personal page too.
While I was off, a post popped up that I thought Wetaskiwin Times followers should be aware of, so I did what I do and shared it.

As soon as I hit share, I got a fairly rude message from Facebook saying the post I had shared was shared by no one in Canada could see it.
And so it has come.
First tabled by the Federal Government in June 2022, Bill C-18 essentially forces companies like Google and Meta, which owns Facebook, to negotiate deals to pay Canadian media companies for the content they link to and preview on their websites and platforms.
Google and its parent company, ABC, say they are working to come up with viable solution so Canadians can still access news while adhering to the spirit of the legislation.
Meta started blocking news content and sharing.
So, if you have been getting your Wetaskiwin Times stories through our Facebook page, I’m going to ask you to go straight to the source — our website (www.wetaskiwintimes.com) and, of course, the paper itself.
There you will see everything to keep you up-to-date on what’s happening in our community every week and don’t forget to check the website to see who came out ahead in the City’s by-election Tuesday if you don’t want to wait until next Wednesday to find out.
