McYikes! Michigan Has the Worst-Rated McDonald’s in America
It comes with the territory. When it comes to fast food, not everyone is always going to be happy.
"I got the wrong order."
"These fries are cold."
"Your lobby is dirty."
Anyone who's ever worked at a McDonald's has probably heard all of those complaints, and many more. There's even an entire Facebook group dedicated to McDonald's Complaints.
There are more than 13,000 McDonald's locations in the United States, including 531 right here in Michigan - and according to ScrapeHero.com, one of our Michigan locations holds the (dis)honor of being the worst-rated McDonald's in America.
Where is the worst-rated McDonald's in America?
ScrapeHero says the McDonald's location at 22333 E. 9 Mile Rd. in the Detroit suburb of St. Clair Shores has the worst ratings of any McDonald's location in the entire United States.
Averaging out the ratings from 1859 customer reviews, this particular St. Clair Shores McDonald's location got a 2.3 on a scale of 5. A McDonald's location in Ohio and a couple in Indiana came close, but no other McDonald's location dipped quite as far down as this one in St. Clair Shores (not to be confused with the better-rated McDonald's location at 31255 Harper Avenue in St. Clair Shores).
Why is this St. Clair Shores location the worst-rated McDonald's in America?
