Datamatics, a global Digital Operations Technology, and experience company, has announced that it has been recognized as a Major Contender by Everest Group for the 6th consecutive year in the Finance and Accounting Outsourcing (FAO) PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025.
Datamatics has improved its scores on both Market Impact (Market Adoption) and Vision & Capabilities, reaffirming its position as a trusted partner for global enterprises.Everest Group has further recognized Datamatics as a Tier 1 player under the IT+ BPS category, underscoring its expanding Finance & Accounting process coverage through FINATO®.
FINATO® is a comprehensive digital suite that delivers AI-powered finance transformation for enterprises, bringing together artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, analytics, and mobility. It unifies core functions across Procureto-Pay (P2P), Order-to-Cash (O2C), Record-to-Report (R2R), Travel & Expense (T&E), Contract Management, and Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A). By integrating these modules into a single intelligent platform, FINATO enables enterprises to streamline operations, ensure compliance, and empower CFOs with real-time insights for faster, data-driven decision-making.
Key strengths of Datamatics FINATO as mentioned in the Everest Group FAO PEAK Matrix® 2025:
- FINATO's AI-driven enhancements across P2P and O2C modules, including AIenabled collections, predictive insights, and low-code/no-code UI with a mobile version for anytime access.
- Datamatics strengthened FINATO by embedding advanced AI/ML functionalities such as AI-enabled vendor evaluation and shortlisting, and AIpowered auto extraction of invoice data in P2P module
- AI capabilities in O2C module include AI-enabled collections and dunning strategies, and agentic workflows in its integrated receivables module
- Strong partnership with Microsoft to build AI-powered solutions such as Copilots for Accounts Payable, Credit Risk Management, Collections, and Cash Applications collaboration with Google, enhancing advanced analytics capabilities for clients.
- Datamatics has notable experience in serving SMB and mid-market clients, with a strong focus on the Manufacturing industry
"Datamatics has enhanced its technology-led F&A services through investments in its modular orchestration platform, FINATO, embedding AI capabilities and agentic workflows across processes including FP&A. Its proprietary digital assets such as TruBot (RPA) and TruBI (analytics), strategic partnerships with leading technology providers to develop F&A Copilots and advanced analytics capabilities, and emphasis on value-driven engagements through flexible pricing constructs have anchored its position as a Major Contender in Everest Group's 2025 Finance and Accounting Outsourcing (FAO) Services PEAK Matrix® Assessment," said Shirley Hung, Partner, Everest Group. "Datamatics' experience in serving SMB and mid-market clients, coupled with its industry-contextualized approach, further differentiates it in the market."
On the occasion, Navin Gupta, EVP & Head of Digital Operations, Datamatics, said, "Our inclusion in the FAO PEAK Matrix® Assessment 2025, for the sixth year in a row, shows Datamatics' strong focus on innovation and client success. We bring together our AI-powered Intelligent Automation platforms and deep domain expertise to deliver scalable, productized, and AI-driven solutions that connect well with customers worldwide." He further added, "Going forward, we will continue to empower clients with advanced digital finance solutions that drive growth, improve efficiency, and create measurable business impact."
Datamatics is consistently ranked amongst the top 10 global vendors for Finance & Accounting by leading industry analysts.
