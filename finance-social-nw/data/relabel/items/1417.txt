Target, Nvidia and American Express have emerged as some of the most desirable workplaces in India in 2025, according to Blind, an anonymous community app used by professionals to share workplace insights.
The list was topped by global tech giants Apple, Google, and Meta.
Conversely, a few companies didn’t fare as well in employee feedback. Among those receiving the lowest ratings were DP World, MindTickle, Paytm and InMobi.
      
                
When it comes to company culture, Target, Nvidia, American Express, Akamai Technologies and ServiceNow had high scores, and stood out for fostering positive working environments.
Similarly, trust in leadership was strongest at Target, American Express, ServiceNow, Arista Networks, AMD and Nvidia, “reinforcing the strong link between these categories and overall employee satisfaction”.
Alongside company ratings, search trends on the Blind platform offered further insights into what Indian professionals are actively exploring. Over the past year, India-based professionals showed the most interest in companies such as Meta, Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Apple, Nvidia, ByteDance, Netflix, OpenAI and Walmart.
Beyond specific companies, popular search terms included “India offer,” “layoff,” “referral,” “promotion,” and “H-1B”.
“Globally, non-monetary factors have consistently shown a stronger correlation with overall satisfaction, while compensation frequently emerges as an outlier. This trend holds true in India as well. Our analysis shows that company culture and management have the strongest ties to overall ratings, while compensation shows the weakest,” said Alex Han, communications manager at Blind.
How the rankings were determined
The analysis was drawn from verified employee reviews submitted by India-based professionals on Blind over the past year. While over 7,000 companies were reviewed, only those with a minimum of 10 reviews were considered for the rankings.
