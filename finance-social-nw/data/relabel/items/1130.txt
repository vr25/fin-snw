Lancaster estate agents make shortlist for prestigious national customer service award
A Lancaster estate agents is celebrating after being shortlisted for a national award.
JDG Estate Agents, based in Market Street, are in line for a Customer Service Award at the prestigious Negotiator Awards.
The Negotiator Customer Service Awards is powered by View Agents, the platform which aggregates customer review scores from Google, Trustpilot, Feefo, Facebook, Apple and Yell and also measures review sentiment, producing a score which reflects a blend of customer feedback and ratings.
JDG said on their Facebook page: "What we love about this, is that like the EA Masters, we haven't had to pay to enter or create a submission, it is purely based on what we do every single day!"
The results are ranked by region and each year the top 20 branches from each region of the UK will be revealed as a shortlist, with all receiving accolades and the top 10 in each region, special recognition.
Three branches by region will then be awarded Gold, Silver and Bronze accolades.
