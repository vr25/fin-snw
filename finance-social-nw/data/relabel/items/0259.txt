New All You Can Eat Sushi Restaurant And 7 Others To Try In Buffalo
If you're really "HUNGRY-hungry," then an all-you-can-eat buffet sounds like Heaven, right? Here are 7 to try here in Buffalo. There is a new all-you-can-eat restaurants that have opened in Western New York. Wind Japanese & Thai offers you as much food as your heart desires.
All to the restaurants are listed in no particular order.
1. Wind Japanese & Thai
7566 Transit Rd, Buffalo, NY 14221
Lunch All You Can Eat: Mon- Thurs: Adult $30.99 - Senior $29.99 - Kids 4 to 10 $10.99 - Under 4 $4 // Fri-Sun: Adults $32.99 - Senior $31.99 - Kids 4 to 10 $10.99 - Under 4 $4
2. The New Jewel Of India - 4.4 Stars
It is located at 1609 Kenmore Ave, Buffalo, NY 14217. The buffet is $13.99 for lunch and $17.99 for dinner.
Robert Lee left a 5-star review on Google,
My family and I love this place!!!! We have never had Indian food before but were so happy we tried it here for the first time it’s was truly phenomenal there was many options for my wife who is vegan and my daughter and I who are vegetarian. The family is also very nice, all in all I really recommend this place it’s absolutely fabulous for family dinner or date night 😊
3. Amherst Buffet - 4 Stars
Amherst Buffet, which serves Asian cuisine, is located at 1551 Niagara Falls Blvd, Amherst, NY 14228 (however their Facebook page says they are moving in July to 2309 Eggert Road in the Sheridan Plaza). The brunch buffet is $10.50, the is lunch buffet is $8.99, and the dinner buffet is $12.99. The Friday and Saturday dinner buffet, along with the Sunday all-day buffet is $15.99.
4. Texas de Brazil - 4.4 Stars
Our menu is a fixed price that includes a continuous dining service for our meats, salad area and side items. So, for one price you can have as many servings as you’d like!
The Brazilian steakhouse is located at 1 Walden Galleria Space P-101, Buffalo, NY 14225. The dinner price is $52.99, salad only is $31.99.
5. Friendly Chinese Buffet - 4.1 Stars
Friendly's is located at 800 Harlem Rd #300, Buffalo, NY 14224. The buffet prices range from $11.99 to $15.99. It has a 4.1-star rating on Google.
Patty Dubz gave it a 5-star rating,
I love coming to this place. As long as you show up at the beginning of lunch or dinner the food is fresh. The people are always polite and nice. And the prices are always good.
6. Buffalo Little Lamb - 4.4 Stars
Buffalo Little Lamb is located at 3188 Sheridan Dr, Amherst, NY 14226. It serves Chinese and Korean dishes. There is a hot pot buffet that is $26 or they offer a brunch buffet for $11.
Mike Graz, a Google local guide, wrote a 5-star review,
Good quality hot pot for good prices with a vast array of sauces to go along with your mix of vegetables and meat. Like the Mala tang broth the most. The spiciness clears out your sinuses
7. Tandoori Hut - 4.6 Stars
Tandoori Hut is located at 3171 Main St, Buffalo, NY 14214. They offer a lunch buffet, but no price is listed on the website.
Joseph Johnson, who left a 5-star rating on Google said,
I had the lunch buffet and let me tell you, you get what you pay for. I am uncomfortably full right now because everything I had was fantastic. It made my day to be able to walk in, grab a plate, and help myself to fantastic food. I didn't have any cold dishes but I'm sure they would have been fantastic. I also didn't have any meat products in my food either (since it is my preference) and I had an abundance of choices. I can't wait to go back. I just have to make room in my stomach, should take me about a week.
8. Bob & John's La Hacienda - 4.3 Stars
Bob & John's serves up Italian cuisine at 1545 Hertel Ave, Buffalo, NY 14216. It offers a pizza and salad bar.
