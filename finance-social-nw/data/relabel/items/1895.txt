Your Go-To Guide for Heart-Shaped Foods, Including Pizza, in Rochester, MN This Year
On Valentine's Day, one of the most anticipated items people expect to receive is a card. Close to 145 million Americans exchange these for this romantic holiday. You can just do the norm and get a card and some chocolate or flowers to show your +1 that you adore them...or you could mix things up a bit.
Thanks to a bunch of creative spots in Rochester, you don't have to go far to find something unique. Quite a few of these local ideas aren't just great for adults...they can work for kids too.

Ultimate Guide To Heart-Shaped Foods (Including Pizza) in Rochester, Minnesota
Because about 36 million heart-shaped boxes of chocolate are sold each year for Valentine's Day, it is obvious that this has been a tried and true gift. You can go with the norm and be like everyone else, or you could mix it up a bit and surprise your true love with something unique instead.
Below are a few heart-shaped ideas that involve food that aren't your typical "box of chocolate" that you can find in the Rochester area:
Dunkin' - this popular donut spot has already kicked off Valentine's Day with their Brownie Batter and Cupid's Choice donuts. You can even get a heart-shaped donut box, Munchkins that are sprinkled with Valentine's themed colors, or specialized 25-cound heart-shaped munchkin tins. Find more details and locations on the Dunkin' website.
Cold Stone Creamery - if you have an ice cream cake fan in your home, Cold Stone is selling a Fudge Truffle Decadence heart-shaped treat you'll want to check out. The photo on their website looks amazing - you can see it here.
Chick-fil-A - a fan favorite is back at the popular chicken spot, Chick-fil-A. Up until Valentine's Day, you can fill a heart-shaped tray with the following: 30 Chick-fil-A nuggets, 10 Chick-n-Minis, 10 Chick-n-Strips, 6 Chocolate Chunk Cookies, or 12 Chocolate Fudge Brownie halves. Find a few more details and Rochester locations here.
Krispy Kreme - this isn't in Rochester but we do finally have a Krispy Kreme location in Minnesota again. If you want to make the drive, you could score some limited-edition Valentine's heart-shaped donuts. See some of the options and find the new Krispy Kreme location in Minnesota here.
Ultimate Guide to Heart-Shaped Pizza in Rochester, MN!
Pizzamarketplace.com reported in 2024 that heart-shaped pizzas are one of the latest trends for those who enjoy a cheesy slice of pie. However, I found out that the heart-shaped pizza tradition has been popping up in various spots since about the 1950s.
If you don't want to attempt to make one yourself, here a few pizza spots in Rochester who typically sell heart-shaped pizza on Valentine's day.
Papa Murphy's - this is the place where you can take it home and bake it! This Valentine's Day, they are featuring their HeartBaker, which is a large pepperoni or cheese pizza that you just take home and bake. 12 to 18 minutes at 425 degrees F and dinner is served. You can order it now on their website.
Godfather's - The last few years, Godfather's had heart-shaped pizzas for the fun holiday. According to cozymeal.com, this heart-shaped pizza hasn't been announced yet but it is expected to be on the menu again this year. Watch the Godfather's website for updates.
Pizza Hut - Looks like your opportunity to buy a heart-shaped pizza at Pizza Hut has already begun. Until February 22nd, you can order a medium 1-topping pizza on hand-tossed crust that is shaped like a heart. See ordering options and the full menu at the Pizza Hut website.
Domino's - I couldn't find anything official yet that says Domino's is indeed bringing back the heart-shaped pizza this year, but everyone is expecting that they will. Watch their website and menu for updates.
I haven't heard any commercials on Y-105FM or noticed any updates about heart-shaped pizzas on the Facebook pages from these local spots, but they may have something pop up too: BB's, Mr. Pizza (North and South), Pi, Rosati's, Pasquale's, Tilda's, Broadway, Red's Savoy Pizza.
