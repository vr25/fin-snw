Oxford Police keeping shoppers safe with patrols on the ground, in the air
Published: Nov. 23, 2025 at 5:08 PM CST|Updated: Nov. 23, 2025 at 5:09 PM CST
OXFORD, Ala. (WBRC) - Oxford Police are getting ready for the holiday shopping season.
Starting this week, police will be patrolling in both marked and unmarked cars.
They will also be keeping shoppers safe while doing surveillance from the air.
“Expect a heavy police presence during the holiday shopping season. Remember, if you see something, say something,” said Chief Bill Partridge in a Facebook post.
