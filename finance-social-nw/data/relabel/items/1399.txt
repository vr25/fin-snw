Africa Cup of Nations started in rather boring fashion with two nil nil draws between South Africa vs Cape Verde and Angola vs Morocco. But as we know it, the goals will be coming and that is the exact reason why you need to get Orange AFCON, SOUTH AFRICA 2013 application. The application is available to download from Google Play and the iTune App Store and is free of charge.
Orange AFCON, SOUTH AFRICA 2013 app brings fans the opportunity to:
watch daily and exclusive video highlights at 00:00 GMT+1
enjoy exclusive video round-ups of top goals at the end of each group day
receive live alerts to keep up to date with lineups, goals and final scores
follow news from all the teams
get full access to fixtures and results for each stage of the event
watch highlights from the Orange AFCON, ANGOLA 2010 and the Orange AFCON, GABON-EQUATORIAL GUINEA 2012
discover the five fantastic South African venues (Johannesburg, Durban, Nelson Mandela Bay, Mbombela and Rustenburg)
share their passion on Twitter and Facebook
participate in the first official Pepsi Fantasy Game from January 19th to February 10th 2013
