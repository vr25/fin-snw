This year we will again have a drive-up service for Ash Wednesday, Feb. 14 from 7 to 8 pm. People will be invited to stay in their vehicles and receive ashes. Lenten devotional materials will be available for pick-up. We thank the Upland Fire Company for volunteering to bring lights and help with the evening.
