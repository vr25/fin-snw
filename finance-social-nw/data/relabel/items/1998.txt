More than 25 establishments across Wales have the worst possible food hygiene rating. The latest list includes a primary school breakfast club and an off-grid wellness destination.
The Food Standards Agency classes businesses with zero stars out of five as needing "urgent" improvement. Council inspectors visit premises and consider how food is handled, stored and prepared.
They also look at how clean facilities are and how food safety is managed. If an inspector finds there is an imminent risk to public health they have the power to stop part of the operation or close it down entirely until the issues are addressed. They can also set a deadline for improvements.
The information below is publicly available on the Food Standards Agency website. The ratings appear as they do on that site at the time of publication. WalesOnline has made efforts to get responses from the businesses listed. If yours would like to add a response to this article, please email conor.gogarty@walesonline.co.uk

Sunflower Fish Bar and Kebab House
- Address: 33 High Street, Merthyr Tydfil
- Business type: Restaurant
- Date of inspection: January 29, 2026
- Hygienic food handling: Improvement necessary
- Cleanliness and condition of facilities and building: Urgent improvement necessary
- Management of food safety: Major improvement necessary
- What the business says: When we called the restaurant, its manager told us: "I don't know the reasons for the score but we have applied for a re-rating. I just started two weeks ago. All the previous places I worked had four or five stars. That's why the owner hired me. I've got the certificates and all these things."
Maison De Masala
- Address: Unit 2B, Parc Tawe, Swansea
- Business type: Restaurant
- Date of inspection: February 24, 2026
- Hygienic food handling: Major improvement necessary
- Cleanliness and condition of facilities and building: Major improvement necessary
- Management of food safety: Urgent improvement necessary
- What the business says: Maison de Masala, which opened last October, bills itself as the biggest and best Indian restaurant in Swansea city centre. It has an average rating of 4.6 stars after 170 Google reviews, and is a sister restaurant to the popular Dark Horse in Mumbles. Asked the reason for the catastrophic hygiene score, a Maison de Masala spokesman said: "The inspection took place as the team was preparing for service, and an unexpected boiler breakdown temporarily affected our hot water supply.
- "This was an isolated incident and not reflective of our usual standards. The boiler was repaired the following day, and we have since reviewed our procedures and equipment to ensure full compliance across all areas. We remain committed to maintaining the highest standards and are confident that this will be reflected in an updated rating following reinspection."
Zozan Kebab House
- Address: 36-38 Vale Road, Rhyl, Denbighshire
- Business type: Takeaway
- Date of inspection: November 26, 2025
- Hygienic food handling: Generally satisfactory
- Cleanliness and condition of facilities and building: Improvement necessary
- Management of food safety: Urgent improvement necessary
- What the business says: Owner Niko Gunes told us the inspector was concerned that parts of the site were in disrepair and that there were holes in the kitchen floor, though he denied there was any rodent infestation as "we have pest control". He said he spent around £35,000 on repairs to address the issues raised but that on returning the inspector remained unimpressed.
- After Mr Gunes had paid around £270 for a quick reinspection, disaster struck midway through it. He had a major disagreement with the Denbighshire council inspector over which book he was meant to be using to log temperatures, and he ordered the officer to leave the premises before the assessment could be completed.
- Since halting the reinspection, Mr Gunes says he has been pleading with the council to give what he would consider a fairer chance at improving the hygiene score, and not to be "mean" about the operation he's running.
- He added that Just Eat and Uber Eats orders have been down by around 50% since the zero, and he fears for the takeaway's future if it does not get a better score soon. "I think I will have no choice but to leave Rhyl," he said.

- Date of inspection: June 4, 2025
- Hygienic food handling: Improvement necessary
- Cleanliness and condition of facilities and building: Major improvement necessary
- Management of food safety: Major improvement necessary
- What the school says: Shortly after the inspection, headteacher Linda Crockett told the Powys County Times: "We are very disappointed by the recent food hygiene rating of zero awarded to our breakfast club following an inspection by the Powys environmental health officer."

We are also working closely with environmental health officers to ensure that all necessary improvements are made swiftly and thoroughly. A reinspection will be requested at the earliest possible opportunity and we are confident that the changes we have implemented will result in a significantly improved rating."
- Despite the stated intention to request a rescore at "the earliest possible opportunity", there has been no further inspection since June last year and the rating remains zero, according to the Food Standards Agency website.

- Date of inspection: June 2, 2025
- Hygienic food handling: Urgent improvement necessary
- Cleanliness and condition of facilities and building: Generally satisfactory

- What the business says: This was the third zero-star hygiene rating the business had received in 18 months. Quick Shop did not respond to our latest approach for comment but, in a previous list of zero-rated businesses, the manager Mo Ali told us the shop had "some problems" which led the inspector to order it to close for two days.

He was speaking weeks before the shop's reinspection, which led to the same disastrous score. A year later there was a third inspection – again resulting in a zero.
The Fox & Hounds
- Address: Park Road, Pontymister, Risca, Caerphilly county
- Business type: Pub
- Date of inspection: November 18, 2024
- Hygienic food handling: Generally satisfactory
- Cleanliness and condition of facilities and building: Improvement necessary
- Management of food safety: Urgent improvement necessary
- What the business says: When we first reported on the pub's zero score, in last year's list, the Fox & Hounds told us: "This is a temporary blip in our otherwise long-standing and well-trusted business. On the day of the inspection, we were in the middle of refurbishment and scaffolding had been erected outside the premises. The tools required for the maintenance had been moved inside owing to the recent storms damaging our outside storage. As a result, tools and materials were temporarily being kept in our store room. Although this room does not house any products which are for sale to the general public, this was identified as an area of concern, and this matter has now been addressed."
- The pub added: "There was also an issue with one carbon dioxide gas bottle not being adequately tethered to the wall, which we were able to remedy immediately. To clarify matters, the business does not serve freshly-made food, only pre-packaged items such as crisps, nuts and sealed containers of alcohol. The business always complies with the recommended stock rotation procedures, ensuring all products served are within date, and all hygiene requirements regarding drinks and snacks are maintained. None of the issues raised were in respect of food or drink hygiene, but rather due to maintenance and safety issues in respect of the building."
