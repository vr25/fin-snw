Followed by a(n alleged) Call of Duty Showcase.
The Xbox Showcase for 2024 will take place on 10 June here in Australia, followed by a further showcase for what’s expected to be the next Call of Duty title set within the Black Ops world.
Following last year’s double feature with Starfield, Microsoft will hold a general showcase from 3.00 am AEST on 10 June, followed by another showcase focusing on one single title. While Microsoft hasn’t confirmed this title as yet, it’s almost certainly Call of Duty.
“This will also be our first Showcase featuring games from our portfolio of studios across Activision, Blizzard, Bethesda and Xbox Game Studios, in addition to titles from our third-party partners,” Microsoft said.
The livestreams will be featured in over 30 languages across a bevy of channels including YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, and more.
